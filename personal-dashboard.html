Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Notion Personal Dashboard show?

The Personal Dashboard gives you a single view of your day and week: upcoming tasks, active goals, habit check-ins, recent notes, scheduled events, and key metrics — your personal command centre in one Notion page.

How do I customise the Personal Dashboard for my workflow?

The dashboard uses linked database views. Rearrange sections, add new views from your existing databases, change filters, and adjust the layout to show exactly what you need to see each day.

Can I link my Personal Dashboard to other Notion databases?

Yes. The dashboard pulls data from your tasks, goals, habits, and notes databases using linked views. As you update those databases anywhere in your workspace, the dashboard reflects the changes automatically.

How do I use the Personal Dashboard for a morning review routine?

Open the dashboard each morning to review today's tasks, check your habit tracker, scan upcoming deadlines, and identify your top three priorities for the day. The goal is a 5-minute review that sets your focus for the entire day.

Is the Notion Personal Dashboard template free?

Yes, completely free. Duplicate it to your Notion workspace and start each day with clarity and focus — no payment required.
