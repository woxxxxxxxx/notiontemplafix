About the Book Tracker Notion Template

The Book Tracker Notion template from NotionTemplaFix gives book lovers, students, and lifelong learners a beautiful, organised system for managing their reading life. Whether you read voraciously across many genres or focus deeply on specific subjects, this template helps you keep track of what you have read, what you want to read, what you are currently reading, and most importantly the ideas and insights you want to remember and use from everything you read.

The template's reading list database allows you to catalogue every book you encounter whether recommended by a friend, mentioned in a podcast, discovered in a bookstore, or found through an article. Each entry stores title, author, genre, source of recommendation, and your reading status. The status tracking system shows your books across four stages: want to read, currently reading, completed, and reference giving you a clear overview of your entire reading pipeline at any time.

The reading notes and highlights section is the most intellectually valuable feature of this tracker. Instead of finishing a book and immediately losing access to the ideas that most resonated with you, this section gives you a dedicated space to capture key quotes, arguments, insights, and applications organised by book and searchable across your entire library. These notes become a personal knowledge database that grows more valuable with every book you add.

Monthly and annual reading goals help you stay intentional about your reading practice, while genre and author statistics give you interesting insights into your reading patterns. This template is completely free to duplicate into your Notion workspace. Start tracking your reading today and never forget a great idea again.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I track with the Notion Book Tracker template?

The Notion Book Tracker lets you log every book you read, want to read, or are currently reading. Track title, author, genre, rating, start and finish dates, notes, and reading status all in one organised database.

Can I filter my reading list by genre or status in this template?

Yes. The template includes pre-built filtered views so you can instantly see books by reading status (read, reading, to-read), genre, rating, or date finished — no manual sorting needed.

How do I record notes and highlights in the Book Tracker?

Each book entry has a dedicated notes section where you can paste quotes, write summaries, record key takeaways, or link to related resources. Your notes are always attached to the book they came from.

Can I set a yearly reading goal with this template?

Yes. The Book Tracker includes a reading goal tracker so you can set an annual target and watch your progress update automatically as you mark books as finished throughout the year.

Is the Notion Book Tracker template free?

Yes, completely free. Download the template, duplicate it to your Notion workspace, and start building your reading library immediately with no payment required.
