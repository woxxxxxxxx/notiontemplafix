About the Goal Tracker Notion Template

The Goal Tracker Notion template from NotionTemplaFix transforms how you set, monitor, and achieve your most important goals. Unlike simple to-do lists or vague intentions, this template guides you through a structured goal-setting and tracking process that research in behavioural science shows dramatically increases the likelihood of goal achievement. From annual objectives to 90-day sprints to weekly milestones, this system works at every time horizon.

The template begins with a goal definition framework that encourages you to articulate not just what you want to achieve, but why it matters to you, what success looks like specifically, and what obstacles you anticipate. This level of clarity at the outset is one of the strongest predictors of goal follow-through, as it keeps you connected to the deeper purpose behind your objectives when motivation naturally fluctuates.

Progress tracking features include milestone checklists that break large goals into manageable steps, a weekly check-in system for reviewing progress and adjusting tactics, and a reflection log for recording insights and lessons learned along the way. The visual progress indicators make your momentum visible, turning goal pursuit from an abstract ambition into a concrete, trackable journey with clear evidence of progress.

Whether you are pursuing professional development, fitness milestones, creative projects, financial goals, or personal growth, this template gives you the structure to go from intention to action. Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of goals can I track with the Notion Goal Tracker?

The Notion Goal Tracker supports personal, professional, health, financial, creative, and any other goal type. Set long-term goals, break them into milestones, and link daily or weekly tasks to track consistent progress.

How do I break down big goals into actionable steps?

Each goal in the tracker has a linked milestones database. Add milestones for each major checkpoint, then attach tasks to each milestone. This turns any large goal into a clear, step-by-step action plan.

Can I track my progress percentage for each goal?

Yes. The template includes a progress field that updates as you complete linked milestones and tasks. You can see at a glance how far along you are for every active goal.

Can I set deadlines and review dates for goals?

Yes. Each goal has a target date, a last review date, and a next review date. The review system helps you stay on track and course-correct before goals drift off schedule.

Yes, completely free. Duplicate it to your Notion workspace and start tracking your goals with clarity and structure — no payment required.
