About the Budget Tracker Notion Template

The Budget Tracker Notion template by NotionTemplaFix gives you a comprehensive personal finance system built entirely within Notion. Instead of switching between multiple apps or maintaining complicated spreadsheets, this template consolidates your income tracking, expense categorisation, savings goals, and financial reporting into one clean, accessible workspace that you control completely.

The template is built around the principle that awareness precedes change. Most people who struggle with their finances are not irresponsible they simply lack a clear, real-time picture of where their money is going. This budget tracker makes your financial reality visible and measurable, giving you the information you need to make better decisions about spending, saving, and investing.

This template is particularly powerful when used consistently month-over-month, as the historical data reveals spending trends, seasonal patterns, and the impact of changes you make to your budget. Frequently Asked Questions

What expenses can I track with the Notion Budget Tracker?

The Notion Budget Tracker lets you record all income sources and expense categories including housing, food, transport, subscriptions, entertainment, and savings — giving you a complete picture of your monthly finances.

How does the Budget Tracker calculate my monthly balance?

The template uses Notion formulas to automatically sum your income and expenses by month, showing your net balance, savings rate, and how actual spending compares to each category budget.

Can I set category budgets and track against them?

Yes. Each spending category has a monthly budget target. The tracker shows actual vs budget for each category so you can spot overspending quickly and adjust before the month ends.

Does the Budget Tracker work for tracking irregular expenses?

Yes. Log one-time and irregular expenses as individual transactions. The tracker categorises and totals them correctly regardless of whether they are recurring or one-off costs.

Is the Notion Budget Tracker template free?

Yes, completely free. Duplicate it to your Notion workspace and start tracking your finances immediately — no payment, no subscription, no account beyond your free Notion workspace.
