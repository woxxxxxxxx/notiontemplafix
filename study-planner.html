About the Study Planner Notion Template

The Study Planner Notion template from NotionTemplaFix gives students, exam candidates, and lifelong learners a structured, effective system for planning, executing, and tracking their study sessions. Effective studying is not simply spending more hours with a textbook it requires strategic planning of what to study and when, use of evidence-based learning techniques like spaced repetition and active recall, regular self-testing to identify knowledge gaps, and consistent reflection on progress. This template builds all of these best practices into your study workflow.

The template includes a subject and topic database that lets you map out everything you need to learn for each course or exam, assign priority levels and estimated study time, and track your progress through each topic. The session planner connects to this database, letting you schedule specific topics for each study session based on your goals, available time, and spaced repetition schedule so you always study the right material at the right time.

The exam preparation module is particularly powerful for students facing high-stakes assessments. For each exam, you can create a complete preparation timeline working backward from the exam date, allocating study time to each topic proportionally to its weight and your current knowledge level. The daily study log records what you covered, how long you studied, and your self-assessed retention building a data record that helps you optimise your approach.

Whether you are preparing for university finals, professional certifications, language exams, or any other learning goal, this Study Planner template gives you the structure to study smarter rather than harder. It is completely free to duplicate into your Notion workspace and start using today.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a study schedule with the Notion Study Planner?

Add your subjects, set exam dates, and block out study sessions in the calendar view. The planner helps you distribute your revision evenly across subjects so you cover everything before each exam.

Can I track my study hours with this template?

Yes. Log each study session with the subject, duration, and topics covered. The template totals your study hours by subject and week so you can see where you are spending your time and adjust as needed.

How does the Study Planner help with revision?

Break each subject into topics, mark each as Not Started, In Progress, or Reviewed, and schedule revision sessions for topics that need more work. This systematic approach ensures nothing is left unrevised before exam day.

Can I set daily study goals in the Study Planner?

Yes. Use the daily planner section to set a study goal for each day — a target number of hours or specific topics to complete. Check off completed sessions to track your daily progress toward your study targets.

Is the Notion Study Planner template free?

Yes, completely free. Duplicate it to your Notion workspace and start studying more effectively and with less stress — no payment required.
