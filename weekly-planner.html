About the Weekly Planner Notion Template

The Weekly Planner Notion template by NotionTemplaFix gives you a complete, structured system for taking control of your week before it begins. Instead of reacting to whatever arrives in your inbox or calendar, this template helps you proactively define your priorities, schedule your time, and set intentions that align with your longer-term goals. Whether you are a student, a professional, a freelancer, or anyone who wants to be more intentional about how they spend their time, this planner provides the structure you need.

The template includes a week-at-a-glance layout that shows your full week in one view, making it easy to spot conflicts, gaps, and opportunities. The top 3 priorities section ensures you always know what matters most, preventing the common trap of staying busy without making meaningful progress. Daily time blocks let you plan each day with precision, while the meal planning and water intake tracker support your physical wellbeing alongside your productivity.

The end-of-week reflection section is one of the most valuable features of this planner. Taking 10-15 minutes each Sunday to review what went well, what could have been better, and what you want to carry into the next week creates a continuous improvement cycle that compounds over time. Users who consistently review their weeks report significantly better follow-through on their goals and a greater sense of control over their time and priorities.

This template is completely free to duplicate into your own Notion workspace. No account or payment is required to get started simply click the link and duplicate the template. It works seamlessly on Notion's free plan and is compatible with all devices where Notion is available.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Notion Weekly Planner help organise my week?

The Weekly Planner gives you a structured view of each day of the week with time blocks, priority tasks, goals for the week, and a reflection section. It helps you plan proactively instead of reacting to whatever comes up.

Can I carry tasks over from one week to the next?

Yes. Tasks that are not completed in one week can be moved or duplicated to the following week. The template makes it easy to review unfinished items during your weekly review and reschedule them intentionally.

How do I do a weekly review using the Planner?

The weekly review section prompts you to reflect on what you accomplished, what did not get done, any lessons learned, and your top three priorities for the coming week. A 15-minute weekly review keeps you aligned with your bigger goals.

Can I link the Weekly Planner to my goals and projects?

Yes. The task sections in the Weekly Planner can use linked database views to pull tasks from your goals or project databases. This ensures your weekly actions are always connected to your longer-term priorities.

Is the Notion Weekly Planner template free?

Yes, completely free. Duplicate it to your Notion workspace and start your week with a clear plan every Monday — no payment required.
