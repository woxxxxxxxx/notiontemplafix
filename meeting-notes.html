About the Meeting Notes Notion Template

The Meeting Notes Notion template from NotionTemplaFix transforms how you capture, organise, and act on the information exchanged in meetings. Research shows that most professionals forget the majority of what was discussed in a meeting within hours and that lack of clear action items and follow-through is the primary reason meetings feel unproductive. This template gives every meeting a consistent structure that ensures nothing important is forgotten and everything agreed is followed through.

The template provides a standardised meeting record that includes meeting date and participants, objectives and agenda, discussion notes organised by topic, decisions made, action items with assigned owners and due dates, and questions or parking lot items for future follow-up. This consistent structure means anyone who reads the notes including yourself weeks later can immediately understand what was discussed and what needs to happen next.

The action items database is linked across all meeting records, creating a single, consolidated view of all outstanding commitments from every meeting you hold. Instead of hunting through individual meeting notes to find what you promised to do, you have one place to review all open action items, sort them by due date or assignee, and mark them complete as they are finished.

Whether you use it for client meetings, team standups, one-on-ones, brainstorming sessions, or project reviews, this template brings professional discipline to your meeting practice. It is free to duplicate into your Notion workspace and immediately start using for your next meeting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Notion Meeting Notes template track?

The Meeting Notes template captures meeting date, attendees, agenda, discussion points, decisions made, and action items with owners and due dates — everything you need to run productive meetings and follow through on outcomes.

How do I assign action items during meetings?

Each meeting note has an action items section with fields for task, owner, and due date. Action items link to your main task database so nothing falls through the cracks after the meeting ends.

Can I search past meeting notes by topic or attendee?

Yes. Notion's full-text search finds any keyword across all your meeting notes instantly. You can also filter the meeting database by date, project, team, or any tag you use to categorise your meetings.

Does the Meeting Notes template support recurring meetings?

Yes. For recurring meetings like weekly stand-ups, create a template within Notion that pre-fills the standard agenda. Duplicate it for each occurrence and update the date and notes — saving setup time every week.

Is the Notion Meeting Notes template free?

Yes, completely free. Duplicate it to your Notion workspace and start running more productive, better-documented meetings — no payment required.
