      Archive Product

      Archiving a product moves it to your Archived Products page instead of your usual Products page.

      This helps de-clutter your usual products page. Your store visitors will still be able to find and purchase archived products on your store. Existing customers will be able to access their product. Existing customers will be able to download their files.

      Archiving is useful when you no longer intend to actively work on a product and just want to move it elsewhere. Out of sight, out of mind.
